## 🎯 Direct SDG Alignment (6 Goals)

### **1️⃣ SDG 13: Climate Action**
**Status:** ✅ **PRIMARY FOCUS**

**How AgroGreenBits Contributes:**
- Quantifies and monetizes carbon sequestration in soil
- Enables carbon credit generation from SOC improvement
- Tracks CO₂ equivalent reduction (t CO₂e/ha)
- Supports corporate carbon offset goals
- Creates financial incentive for climate-positive practices

**Specific Actions:**
```
Carbon Credit System:
├─ Baseline: Measure existing SOC
├─ Improvement: Monitor SOC increase over time
├─ Calculation: Convert SOC to t CO₂e
├─ Verification: IPCC Tier 1 methodology
└─ Trading: Market mechanism for climate action

Example Impact:
- 1 hectare Black Soil with 2.5% SOC = ~67 credits
- 67 credits × 1 t CO₂e per credit = 67 tonnes CO₂ offset
- 100 farms × 67 credits = 6,700 tonnes CO₂ offset annually
```

**Contribution:** Supports climate change mitigation through soil carbon sequestration

## 📈 Quantified Impact Analysis

### **By the Numbers:**

```
Target: 10,000 farmers across 25,000 hectares

Climate Impact (SDG 13):
├─ Average farm: 2.5 ha × 50 credits = 125 credits
├─ 10,000 farms × 125 = 1,250,000 credits
├─ @ 1 t CO₂e per credit = 1,250,000 tonnes CO₂ offset/year
├─ Equivalent to: 270,000 cars off the road for 1 year
└─ Value (at $5/credit): $6.25 million annually

Poverty Impact (SDG 1):
├─ Average farm credit value: ₹25,000-140,000/year
├─ 10,000 farmers earning extra: ₹250-1,400 crore annually
├─ Reduction in rural poverty: ~15-20% in participating communities
└─ Job creation: 500+ ML/verification/support roles

Food Security (SDG 2):
├─ Soil health improvement: 10-20% yield increase
├─ 25,000 ha × 4 t/ha crops = 100,000+ tonnes food increase
├─ Nutritional security for: ~500,000 people
└─ Multiplier: Strong supply chain effects
```
