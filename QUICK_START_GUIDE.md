## 📱 How to Use

### Change Language
1. Look for dropdown in **bottom-right corner**
2. Select your preferred language
3. UI updates instantly ✨
4. Your choice is saved automatically

### Start Meditation
1. Click **"Meditation"** in navigation
2. Choose duration (5, 10, 15, or 30 min)
3. Click **"▶️ Start Meditation"**
4. Watch beginner video or try exercises
5. Timer tracks your session
6. Minutes saved to dashboard

### Try Exercises
1. Scroll down in Meditation section
2. See 6 exercise cards with images:
   - 🫁 Deep Breathing
   - 🧘 Neck & Shoulder Stretch
   - 🧘‍♀️ Child's Pose
   - 🚶 Mindful Walking
   - 🧠 Body Scan Meditation

## 🎓 Exercise Guide

### 🫁 Deep Breathing
**Time**: 5-10 minutes  
**Benefits**: Stress relief, better focus  
**How**: 4-4-4 breathing pattern (inhale-hold-exhale)

### 🧘 Neck & Shoulder Stretch
**Time**: 2-3 minutes  
**Benefits**: Release tension, improve posture  
**How**: Gentle side-to-side tilts and rotations

### 🧘‍♀️ Child's Pose
**Time**: 30 seconds - 1 minute  
**Benefits**: Spinal relaxation, calming  
**How**: Kneel and stretch arms forward

### 🚶 Mindful Walking
**Time**: 5-10 minutes  
**Benefits**: Mental clarity, stress relief  
**How**: Slow walking with focus on breathing

### 🧠 Body Scan Meditation
**Time**: 10-15 minutes  
**Benefits**: Progressive relaxation, awareness  
**How**: Mental scan from head to toe

### 💧 Hydration Break
**Time**: 2-3 minutes  
**Benefits**: Maintains focus, health  
**How**: Mindfully drink water between exercises

### Timer Not Working?
1. Ensure JavaScript is enabled
2. Check browser console for errors
3. Try closing/reopening app
